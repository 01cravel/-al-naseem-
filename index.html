Why now

Six reasons this isn't going to wait for you.

01

The government won't let it fail.

Hudayriyat isn't a private developer's gamble. The whole island is owned and developed by Modon — a company wholly owned by ADQ, Abu Dhabi's sovereign holding company. ADQ controls more than $250 billion in assets. The same entity that built Yas Island, Saadiyat, and Etihad is behind this. They are not in the business of letting their flagship project decline.

Modon · 100% owned by ADQ Sovereign Fund
02

Every dirham of profit is yours.

Buy this villa in London and you'd hand the taxman up to 28% on the way out. Buy it here and you keep all of it. No capital gains tax. No property tax. No income tax on rental yield. No inheritance tax. The UAE has zero plans to introduce any of these for residential property — and a constitutional commitment to the model that built Dubai.

0% · The most tax-efficient property market on earth
03

The market just hit a record.

In Q1 2026 alone, Hudayriyat recorded AED 11.97 billion in property transactions. That's more than Saadiyat (AED 8.8B) and Al Reem (AED 9.45B) combined. An island most international buyers hadn't heard of two years ago is now the single most active real estate market in the entire emirate. Money knows where it's going.

04

You're getting Dubai prices in 2018.

The villa we're showing you costs AED 1,279 per square foot. Dubai Hills Estate — comparable luxury, older development — sells for AED 3,400. Saadiyat trades at AED 2,950. You're buying frontline coastal Abu Dhabi at a 30–60% discount to comparable stock that's already sold out elsewhere. That gap closes. It always closes.

The hype is just getting started.

Bulgari opening a hotel next door. Surf Abu Dhabi — the world's largest wave pool — already open. MGM's anticipated casino licence approval in Abu Dhabi will be a region-defining event. ADGM (the financial district) is doubling in size. A high-speed rail to Dubai breaks ground next year. None of this is priced in yet.

10-year residency, included.

The villa comes with an automatic UAE Golden Visa — 10 years of residency for you, your spouse, and your children. No income requirements, no renewals, no minimum stay. Open UAE bank accounts. Move your business here. Send your children to school here. Or just have the option for when you want it. Comparable visas elsewhere cost £2M+ to obtain on their own.
